King Chung (Johnny) Ho

Machine learning researcher & engineer

I work on machine learning for healthcare. I'm currently at Edwards Lifesciences. Before that I was at Verily, building digital measures of Parkinson's disease from wearable sensors and video. I earned my Ph.D. at UCLA, where I applied deep learning to medical imaging for acute ischemic stroke.

Outside work I build game-playing agents and write about what I learn along the way in the blog.
