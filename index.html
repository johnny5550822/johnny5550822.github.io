King Chung (Johnny) Ho

“Don't just aspire to make a living; aspire to make a difference.”

I’m an AI researcher and engineer passionate about building intelligent systems that learn, reason, and solve real-world problems. My interests span foundation models, multimodal learning, reinforcement learning, and autonomous AI agents. I’m particularly excited about turning cutting-edge AI research into systems that actually work.

Currently, I’m a Senior Principal AI Engineer and tech lead at Edwards Lifesciences, building AI-powered medical imaging systems and image foundation models. Previously, at Verily (Google Life Sciences), I developed multimodal foundation models trained on over 1.6 million hours of wearable sensor data. I earned my Ph.D. from UCLA, where I researched deep learning for stroke imaging.

Outside of work, I explore emerging AI research by building things from scratch. I share my experiments, discoveries, and occasional failures on my blog.
