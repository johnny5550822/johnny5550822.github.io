King Chung (Johnny) Ho

“Don't just aspire to make a living; aspire to make a difference.”

I'm an applied AI/ML researcher and engineer. Most of my work is machine learning that has to hold up outside the lab, where labels are scarce, data arrives messy from many sites, and the people affected by a model's output are patients.

Today I'm a Senior Principal AI Engineer and tech lead at Edwards Lifesciences, where my team builds the imaging platform and image foundation models behind AI-assisted medical imaging. Before that I spent five years at Verily (Google Life Sciences) training multimodal foundation models on more than 1.6 million hours of wearable sensor data. I did my Ph.D. at UCLA on deep learning for stroke imaging.

I keep up with the research frontier by building things myself. My current independent project is an agent for competitive Pokémon battles that combines imitation learning, search and reinforcement learning: a small lab for questions about planning when much of the game state is hidden. I write about it on the blog.
